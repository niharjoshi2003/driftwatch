Discovery scraper: one pricing URL in, one row per visible tier. Fields: vendor (REQUIRED every row, short brand e.g. Linear), tier_name (card heading), tier_price_monthly (number; empty for Contact sales/Custom), currency (ISO), billing_period (monthly|annual), features (bullets on that card only), is_custom_pricing (true if no public number), scraped_at (ISO). Do not invent tiers. Do not convert annual to monthly unless monthly is shown. Never omit vendor.
